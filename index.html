About

I love solving maths and discovering its beauty while doing research on it.

I am a member of Xiao Huang's AI Lab (PolyU X AI Lab) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and I am pursuing early graduation. I study applied mathematics, majoring in Data Science and Analytics.

I was previously in the Department of Applied Mathematics, advised by Prof. Xiaoqi Yang, and later in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, advised by Prof. Binyan Jiang. My capstone is supervised by Prof. Korris Chung Fu Lai. My undergraduate research is supervised by Prof. Xiao Huang.

Foundations I keep close: analysis, algebra, statistics — especially inference, statistical machine learning, and high-dimensional analysis — and optimization.
