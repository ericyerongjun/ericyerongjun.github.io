About Me

I am a member of Xiao Huang's AI Lab (PolyU X AI Lab) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and a research intern with the TMLR Group at Hong Kong Baptist University. I am pursuing early graduation.

I am contributing to the K-Cube research project as project manager and front-end software engineer.

I read applied mathematics as an undergraduate, and I care most about work where a claim can be established rather than merely observed to hold.

I have strong fundamentals in analysis, algebra, and optimization. I'm not a very statistics person but I'm still strong in statistical learning and high dimensional data analysis.

Research

I work toward machine reasoning that can be checked rather than trusted. My primary direction pairs large language models with the Lean proof assistant, so that a model’s reasoning yields an artifact a verifier can accept or reject outright. Around this I study multimodal reasoning, where the evidence for a claim is spread across text, diagrams, and structure, and reinforcement learning as the mechanism for improving reasoning policies against verifiable rewards.

I am also drawn to AI for partial differential equations, where physical law supplies a ground truth a model cannot argue with, and to the infrastructure that makes work at this scale possible.
